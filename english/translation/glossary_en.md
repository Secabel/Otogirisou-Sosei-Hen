# Glossary EN — Otogirisou: Sosei Hen (PSX), Japanese → English

Source of truth is the PSX Japanese (column `japones_corregido` of `translation_en.csv`). The SNES English patch (ButThouMust/Guest) is used ONLY for names and terms. The Spanish glossary (`glossary_psx_es.md`) lists the same terms with the same readings; the decisions below are the English ones.
Rule: every new recurring term (place, object, nickname, catchphrase) is added here BEFORE continuing with the next batch.

## Honorifics and address
- Keep -san, -kun, -chan, -sama as in the Japanese. `[NAME]` already contains its honorific: never add one next to it.
- Avoid "Master/Lord" unless the Japanese says 殿様 (the lord) / 旦那.

## Source misreadings (fixed in `japones_corregido`; kept for reference)
The raw CSV column `japones` comes from an OCR glyph map; `tables/id_fixes_en.json` corrects 34 glyph ids (屋敷, 冗談, 復讐, 匂い, 頬, 紙, 熱湯, 冷蔵庫, 咲, 懐, 姉, 斬, 売, 拠, 酔, 緊, 雑, 稲, 豊, 賓, 荘, 況, 譲, 愕, 殴, 籐, 旦, 徹, 恵, 収, 涼, 巻, 跡…). Remaining doubts: 0x74c 盃 ("ミズ着…ミズ盃！" wordplay) and 0x751 鍋 ("18万人もの少女を鍋にした犯人"); 蹴 in "ナベにけつまづいて". `■` = unreadable glyph; deduce from context.
